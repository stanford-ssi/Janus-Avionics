# TPS7A4501 Notes

## Application Notes

** Output Capacitor Selection**
"A minimum output capacitor of 10 μF with
an ESR of 3 Ω or less is recommended to prevent oscillations

The X5R and X7R dielectrics result in more stable characteristics and are more suitable for use as the output capacitor.

The X7R type has better stability across temperature, while the X5R is less
expensive and is available in higher values."

**Note For Our Use Cases**
We need high thermal stability. Either pick X7R components or just buy massive bulk output capacitors and figure out how to test temperature variant equivalence without dragging output voltage.
